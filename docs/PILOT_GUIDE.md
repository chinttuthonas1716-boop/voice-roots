# Voice Roots: Controlled Pilot Release Guide & Checklist

**Project**: *Voice Roots: Rooting Oral Languages in Text with AI*  
**Scope**: First Controlled Pilot with Consenting Speakers & Community Elders  
**Guiding Principle**: Indigenous Data Sovereignty (OCAP — Ownership, Control, Access, Possession)

---

## 1. Pilot Readiness & Ethics Protocols

Before collecting or testing any community recordings:
1. **Affirmative Speaker Consent**: Every speaker must explicitly confirm consent before recording. No audio may be stored without an affirmative opt-in.
2. **AI Opt-In Separation**: Speech recognition (AI transcription) requires a separate consent checkbox. A speaker may choose to archive audio without AI processing.
3. **Master Audio Immutability**: The speaker's acoustic recording is permanently retained as an immutable master. Transcripts and translations exist strictly as non-destructive annotation layers.
4. **Sacred Knowledge Restriction**: Sacred, secret, or clan-restricted oral knowledge must **never** be uploaded to the open corpus (strictly rejected under OCAP protocols).
5. **Right to Withdraw**: A speaker holds perpetual ownership over their voice and may withdraw consent at any time via the withdrawal endpoint (`POST /api/v1/transcripts/{id}/withdraw-consent`).

## 2. Concise Pilot Tester Checklist

Testers and community coordinators should follow this 5-step checklist for each testing session:

- [ ] **Step 1: Environment & Health Verification**
  - Verify that the local Voice Roots website is running at `http://127.0.0.1:5173`.
  - Confirm backend health at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/health` (`status: healthy`).

- [ ] **Step 2: Speaker Onboarding & Consent**
  - Open **Tab 1: Oral Studio & Archive**.
  - Explain the purpose of the platform to the speaker.
  - Check the affirmative consent checkbox: *"I confirm speaker consent to archive this recording under OCAP data sovereignty protocols."*
  - If the speaker approves AI assistance, check: *"I opt-in to automated speech transcription for this recording."*

- [ ] **Step 3: Recording or File Upload**
  - Select the speaker's language (e.g., Gondi, Koya, Santhali, Telugu, Hindi, English).
  - Specify the regional dialect if known (e.g., *Adilabad*, *Bhadrachalam*).
  - Record 15–60 seconds of speech using the microphone or upload a clean WAV/MP3 file.
  - Click **Save & Archive Audio**. Verify that the recording appears in the Archive table.

- [ ] **Step 4: Transcription & Elder Review Verification**
  - If consented, inspect the generated transcript in the Studio workspace.
  - Compare generated text against the authentic speech audio.
  - If the language is an unwritten oral tongue (Gondi, Koya, Santhali, Lambadi), verify that machine translation is blocked with the required elder guardrail message:  
    `"Translation restricted: Language '...' is an unwritten oral heritage tongue. Automated AI translation without community elder review is prohibited to prevent linguistic hallucination."`
  - Submit any human corrections via the **Correction Audit Trail** form.

- [ ] **Step 5: Resource Search & Semantic Grounding**
  - Open **Tab 2: Semantic Vector Resources**.
  - Search for traditional terms or cultural lore (e.g., *medicinal plant*, *sacred mahua tree*).
  - Verify that retrieved records display genuine institutional attribution, source URLs, and open licenses (`CC BY 4.0`, `CC BY-SA 4.0`, `CC0`).
