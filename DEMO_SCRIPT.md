## 🛡️ Reviewer Q&A Quick Defense

- **Q: Why not just use Google Translate?**  
  *A: Standard models erase tribal and regional dialects. Voice Roots uses fine-tuned models (IndicTrans2, Whisper-Indic) that preserve culturally specific terms like sacred root names and kinship markers.*
- **Q: Why show the native transcript above the translation?**  
  *A: Replacing the mother tongue with English is digital linguistic erasure. The original spoken word remains the permanent ground truth; translation is an optional accessibility layer.*
- **Q: How does the responsive system work?**  
  *A: We use fluid CSS `clamp()`, container queries, and strict 44px minimum touch targets instead of device-specific user-agent sniffing.*
